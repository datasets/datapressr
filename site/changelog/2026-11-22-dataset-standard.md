title: A machine-checkable standard for Commons datasets
---

DataPressr now has a written standard for datasets in the Commons (github.com/datasets, shown at datahub.io/core) and a checker that enforces it: open licence, clear source, declared upkeep (frozen snapshot or living with a refresh job), a "What stands out" note, a page that renders on DataHub, and freshness for living datasets. New datasets run it on every push; a nightly job checks every dataset in the org and keeps a report. Standard: https://github.com/datasets/datapressr/blob/main/docs/dataset-standard.md
