We now run the story skill blind on real questions, have a model from another vendor critique the results, and turn the lessons into skill edits that we re-test on new questions, with the owner's blind preference as the measure that counts. The [story lab page](../docs/story-lab.md) explains what we optimise, how the loop works and what the first rounds have shown, including the first open-data story run (WWII) and a France A/B now awaiting the owner's blind judgement.
