title: Installed skills now work outside the DataPressr repo
promote: false
---

After `npx skills add datasets/datapressr`, `init` now scaffolds a dataset with its conventions file and validator in an empty folder, and `validate` runs the real checks instead of improvising them. The skills carry what they need and link to everything else by full URL, and `capture` files ideas in your own project (Beads, its issues or a local `INBOX.md`) rather than in DataPressr's inbox.
