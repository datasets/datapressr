## R1: retain provisionally — investigate a reader-relevant comparison

**Decision and scope.** Retain the existing R1 wording in `skills/story/SKILL.md` as a hypothesis-backed editorial prompt. Do not promote it as a tested improvement, require contrarian findings, or add mandatory subgroup analysis. No wording change is proposed in this decision.

**Why retain it.** The accepted [owner research](../../../../docs/research/data-story-research.md), at owner commit `2e411017853a15746b45012d4e8ac0b6f8b345d8`, prioritizes concrete questions and clear arguments. The assessed `jbm-london` example in [exemplar notes](../../../../docs/research/data-story-exemplar-notes.md) motivates investigating representativeness while distinguishing regional GDP from household welfare; its historical numerical result remains unreproduced. This provides a craft rationale, not causal evidence from this pilot. R1 is compatible with those useful readings but did not improve the frozen H1 outcome.

**Failure evidence and limit.** [M30755c stage 3](readings/M30755c-stage3.json), `manual_dimensions[0]` and G2, separates useful interest from the unsupported implication that high revenue rules out a revenue problem. [Mb8941a](readings/Mb8941a-stage3.json) adds a government-scope mismatch. All four France outputs retain inferential G2 failures; the causal oil commission remains incompletely answered. A reader-relevant question is insufficient if its answer overstates the evidence. This is not evidence that R1 caused overstatement.

**Next bounded test.** On one independently prepared fresh story case, freeze a commission-level answerability record before writing: the question, permissible comparison, evidence needed for the proposed payoff and what remains unanswerable. In a future authorized one-rule contrast, success requires a masked reader to recover an accurate payoff and comparison while explicitly distinguishing the commission's unsupported remainder; every central inference must clear its separately scoped evidence check. Record descriptive-only and unsupported readings as failures, including an interesting but unsupported answer. Freeze replication and decision thresholds before generation; one new case alone will not establish transfer broadly.

## R3: revise for the next candidate — explain relationships within verified scope

**Decision and scope.** Revise the next candidate's R3 specification to make the evidence boundary concrete at the claimed relationship. The proposed addition is: distinguish an accounting identity, a descriptive association and an externally attributed mechanism; match the units, population and series before linking findings; state a narrower conclusion where the packet cannot establish the stronger one. For a debt ratio, do not present a deficit identity as a complete account of ratio change. For oil, do not silently equate an expiring futures contract with the observed spot-price series. This is a proposed, unimplemented revision; the existing skill bytes remain unchanged. Keep the original synthetic amount/share example and do not require a manufactured transition or remove adequately verified external explanations.

**Why revise it.** R3's source rationale remains sound: the owner's R2.3/R2.5 questions distinguish explanation from adjacent statistics, and assessed `bostock-example` supports an explanatory sequence rather than disconnected facts. Nevertheless, the frozen H3 primary results provide no observed improvement. Across all 51 nested relationships there are 36 supported, 6 unsupported, 8 unassessable and 1 left to the reader. The three unsupported relationships in each France arm, and four unassessable mechanisms in each oil arm, show that relational fluency has not resolved source and inference boundaries.

**Failure evidence and limit.** [Mb8941a's G1/G2](readings/Mb8941a-stage3.json) and relationship `Mb8941a-R7` mix central-government growth rates with general-government claims. [M1d3194's spontaneous reading](readings/M1d3194-stage2.json), its stage 3 limits and the frozen primary selection distinguish the intelligible deficit link from the stronger causal lede. [M948761's G1/G3](readings/M948761-stage3.json) and [Mee832a's G1/G2](readings/Mee832a-stage3.json) leave external causal verification unresolved. Named attribution is neither automatically false nor verified. The revision targets demonstrated failure modes; it is not a claim that R3 introduced them or that additional wording will fix them.

**Next bounded test.** Before a new writer comparison, independently assemble one admissible mechanism source and one accounting/ratio example with exact scope anchors; include deliberate scope-switch and causal-overstatement negatives. Freeze a revised diagnostic that separately assesses the central causal lede and the first eligible descriptive relationship. It must reject the known negative examples while accepting a correctly qualified attribution; this is development validation, not held-out detection. Then preregister a contrast of current candidate versus R3-only revision on one new independently prepared topic, with all other instructions held fixed. Success requires understandable links and no unsupported central causal claim, with separate writing/interest judgments and unchanged validity gates. No calls or skill changes are authorized by this document.
