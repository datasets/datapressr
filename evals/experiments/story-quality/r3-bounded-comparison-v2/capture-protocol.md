# Pre-output delivery transformation contract

This is a documentation contract, not an implemented renderer or completed freeze. Execution remains blocked until an independent reviewer accepts a completed, hashed freeze record **before either writer launches**. Do not fill missing technical identities after observing outputs. Use one existing render path for both arms; no second runner or renderer implementation is authorized here.

The pre-output record must identify the exact renderer/browser executable and version, template/CSS/font/asset hashes, dependency/lock identity, launch flags, locale, timezone, device pixel ratio (1), zoom (100%), viewport widths (390 mobile and 900 desktop), deterministic height/wait/font/image-ready conditions, and network policy. Test the selected path offline with exposed/synthetic material. Missing supported render path or unresolved transformation behavior prevents generation. The completed record is separately pinned and reviewed; this file alone cannot certify it.

Selection rule: use the sole story artifact selected by the frozen existing writer-output contract, plus all assets referenced by that artifact. Before generation, record that contract's exact path/key and the resolver rule. Zero or multiple eligible story artifacts is missing delivery, with no discretionary selection of the strongest passage or alternate story. Preserve the raw story and referenced asset bytes and hashes. No spelling, prose, number, chart, label, title or caption improvements are permitted during delivery.

Render the complete selected artifact in source order. Freeze the selected renderer's exact frontmatter mapping before generation: one title from the designated title field, suppressed duplicate rendering of that same field, and the predefined behavior when that field is absent. Do not choose among titles after reading an output. Frontmatter metadata and author friction notes stay in the administrative record unless the frozen output contract explicitly makes them reader-facing; the freeze record must name their exact syntax and deterministic inclusion/exclusion rule. If that syntax or rule cannot be resolved before generation, stop. Ordinary body text and visible chart captions remain intact; hidden/alt text is not inserted as visible assistance. Do not repair a writer's duplicate title in body prose: retain and score it as a delivery defect.

Use identical template, margins, typography and responsive chart rules for both arms, fixed in the freeze record. Charts must follow the selected renderer's frozen responsive CSS at each width, with no arm-specific enlargement, replacement or relabeling. Record every transformation from original chart bytes to rendered pixels. If the renderer cannot deliver a chart, retain the failure; do not substitute a different chart or source format. Render failures remain factual render outcomes or missing-delivery outcomes, not grounds for rescue editing.

At each width capture the complete full-page PNG after the frozen readiness conditions pass. Mobile delivery consists of consecutive top-to-bottom crops of that PNG with x=0, width=390, y=0,900,1800,... and height=min(900, remaining pixels); no omitted or overlapping rows, no semantic recropping and no resampling. Desktop capture width is 900 and remains verifier evidence. Retain full-page and crop hashes, dimensions, crop coordinates/order, renderer logs and all input hashes. Reject nonmatching width, clipping caused by capture, incomplete fonts/images, missing paragraphs/captions or omitted charts. Preserve the failed capture and reason. No second render attempt with altered settings after inspecting arm results; an infrastructure retry would require a separately reviewed protocol change before generation.

Before packet release, an independent operator checks completeness and equality of transformations against the raw output and frozen recipe. `prepareReaderPacket` receives only the ordered mobile PNG crops with neutral artifact identity; preserve its manifest/hash and the exact delivered image bytes. Keep source, HTML, SVG, alt text, administrative expected answers and treatment mapping out of reader access. Preserve actual viewer delivery dimensions/scaling and any preprocessing evidence independently of the reader's assertions. If intended-size delivery cannot be established, mark readability unresolved rather than passed.

Material changes to story, assets, recipe or capture invalidate downstream packet/render reviews and response linkage; do not reuse a stale review. Retain both successful and failed delivery records. No output or capture exists in this preparation, and no reader/render success is claimed.
