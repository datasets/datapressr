## Frozen future concern-hit rule

The historical rule was “same problem raised anywhere”, with partial matches counted as misses. Retain that conservative rule prospectively and make the evidence auditable:

| Label | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| hit | A critique frozen before owner feedback identifies the same substantive problem in the same passage/chart or clearly specified class, with enough detail to distinguish it from a generic complaint. Example: “the hollow/filled key makes the year unclear” matches year decoding; “simplify the chart” does not. |
| partial | Touches the area but misses the problem, cause or affected comparison. Counts as a miss in the headline hit fraction, but remains visible separately. |
| miss | No specific matching issue, including praise of the very feature the owner reports confusing. Record contradictory praise separately. |
| unassessable | Owner concern is ambiguous or no relevant artifact/critique survives. Exclude with reason from the assessable denominator and report the count; never silently drop a difficult miss. |

Before seeing critic results, a separate coder turns future verbatim owner remarks into concern units, recording what would constitute a match and which artifact was judged. Split distinct issues consistently; preserve the original remark and compound facets so segmentation cannot inflate success. Then an independent matcher compares the frozen critique and concern register, with exact evidence locators. Any adjudicated change retains original coding and reason. Report assessable unique concerns by story/topic, hit/partial/miss/unassessable counts, duplicate owner mentions and exposure status; repeated critiques/renders do not multiply owner concerns. The current candidate author has already seen every historical concern and cannot perform that future blind matching on these eleven.

**False positives:** another concern absent from owner comments is `owner-unassessed`, not false. An actual false-positive claim needs evidence contradicting the alleged defect, a verified renderer artifact, an out-of-scope penalty contrary to the frozen commission, or an explicit owner rejection of that particular allegation. Record the evidence basis and whether it is a technical/scoping error versus a disagreement in taste. This exercise documents known renderer/scoping errors; it cannot estimate precision or a false-positive rate from non-exhaustive owner comments.

**Attribution and scope:** do not automatically discard a named, permitted source explanation because the local CSV contains only outcomes. Code the observable question separately from the attributed mechanism: `answered-by-attribution` records source, reach and verification status; it does not establish causal identification. If the commission forbids external evidence, record that constraint and the provenance problem separately. No runtime v2 answer enums change in this task.
