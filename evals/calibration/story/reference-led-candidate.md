## Evidence gates precede editorial disposition

Record each gate as `pass`, `fail`, `unresolved` or `not-applicable`, with a claim/asset locator, observed evidence and the check needed. A pass applies only to the named checked scope. An inaccessible source is unresolved, not false; a retrieved table is not a reproduced article. Historical harness passes retain their original checker/version and cannot establish a broader gate pass today.

| Gate | Required evidence | Failure and next action |
|---|---|---|
| G1 — source and scope | Source, vintage, population, measure, units, denominator, comparison boundaries and attribution for every central claim; documented use/redistribution basis for material being reused. | Missing or mismatched support blocks that claim's reuse. Pin/retrieve the input; revise the claim or state the gap. Do not infer copying permission from public access. |
| G2 — factual and analytical validity | Recompute central differences/ratios, distinguish percentage points and reversed percentages, subset/whole, nominal/real/share, stocks/flows; causal language fits identification or explicit attribution. | A material wrong number, false total, denominator error or causal overreach requires repair regardless of appeal. Preserve the appealing question while correcting its answer. |
| G3 — reproducibility | For a produced DataPressr story: pinned input and transform, unchanged input, offline rebuild, output hashes and named applicable deterministic checks. | Broken/missing reproduction requires repair or verification. External exemplars without scripts are unresolved here, not presumed reproducible or automatically poor writing. |
| G4 — delivered visual integrity | Actual delivered render at reading size; title, axis, legend, marker and annotations correspond to data. Inspect for clipping, invalid geometry and misleading scaling; check the renderer itself. | Broken or materially misleading assets require repair. Unseen charts/interactions remain unresolved. SVG/source labels alone do not prove a reader can decode a picture. |

`fail` means `repair-required`; unresolved central evidence means `verification-required`; only a checked pass permits editorial review for the checked scope. These are diagnostic dispositions, not publication authorization. No score total, mean or preference can clear a failed gate. Keep all editorial observations even when a gate fails: a compelling article can contain an invalid comparison, and a correct article can be dull. Cosmetic preference alone is an editorial concern, not automatically a factual gate failure.

## Seven anchored manual dimensions

Use `0`, `1`, `2`, or `null` when unassessable. Each rating needs a specific passage/chart/state, an anchor and a limitation. For a bounded excerpt, name that scope and never present its rating as the whole article's score. Choose the lower supported anchor when between anchors; record the plausible adjacent rating and why if uncertain. Do not sum these dimensions. `2` means a demonstrated feature, not universal excellence or factual approval.

| Manual dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| interest | Generic subject or dataset tour; no concrete reader payoff. | Relevant question, but only scale/description or an unstated implication. | Specific consequential puzzle or revealing comparison makes the reader revise an assumption or understand an everyday concern. |
| findings_depth | Lists available values/categories without a finding. | Identifies a pattern or large category but leaves its contents, contribution to change or meaningful comparison to the reader. | Computes the relevant contrast; unpacks the category/distribution carrying the argument; shows what changes and by how much within the available evidence. |
| argument_counterevidence | No answer, or the conclusion ignores conflicting evidence. | Clear answer but a material alternative, boundary choice or affected group is omitted. | Claim and sequence connect evidence to answer; strongest accessible alternative or counterexample is tested; beneficiary/spillover and metric limits sit where they change interpretation. |
| explanation | Technical/source labels stand in for meaning, or asserted cause stands in for evidence. | Defines the pattern/category but leaves a central mechanism unclear, or offers attribution without distinguishing it from what the local data tests. | Explains what the measure means and why the pattern could arise using shown evidence or named, scoped attribution; separates observation, mechanism and uncertainty. A demonstration can explain a mechanism without making a news or causal population claim. |
| chart_design | Unreadable or misleading encoding/denominator; visual conclusion contradicts the claimed comparison. | Useful evidence but decoding, scaling, comparison or annotation requires effort/inference. | Actual inspected visual makes its intended comparison readable at a glance; units, years, encodings and benchmark are recoverable at reading size; precise and first-impression readings agree. |
| writing | Jargon, overprecision, repetition or generic prose hides the point. | Understandable but a key term, magnitude, implication or paragraph still needs work. | Plain and economical; terms and magnitudes are concrete; comparison is stated; necessary method/limits sit beside the relevant claim without crowding the answer. |
| reader_coverage | Main in-reach reader question unanswered. | Main question partly answered, with important in-reach follow-ups open. | Main question and prioritized in-reach follow-ups answered; genuine evidence gaps identified once, with attribution or next evidence needed where useful. |

The scorecard does not require a causal explanation for every descriptive piece, one chart per number, a fixed chart count or a particular palette. Appropriate depth depends on the commission and data. Do not award depth just for naming subgroups, explanation just for adding a caveat, charts just for a legend's existence, or interest just for a prestigious byline.

## Read twice, for different evidence

First record a brief chart-only reading: conclusion, direction of change, unit/denominator and which marks are which year/group. Then inspect labels/data and record what had to be decoded or calculated. A first reading contradicted by the second is a comprehension risk to investigate, even when the analyst can eventually recover the intended answer. Record observed owner confusion separately from an analyst's prediction of confusion; this manual exercise cannot measure a reader's actual five-second response.

Write reader questions before examining the draft, using the commission, intended audience and permitted evidence. Classify each as answerable from inputs, answerable only by permitted attributed context, or outside scope. A fixed-data writer must not be penalized for forbidden data acquisition; missing evidence remains visible as a limit on the answer. In open mode, an omitted obtainable source may be a research weakness. A caveat can be honest and still leave an editorial question open. Distinguish honesty about a gap from explanation of it.

The next comparison must report gates, anchored dimensions, concrete missed findings, disagreement and uncertainty separately. A reviewed procedure on exposed material establishes that its vocabulary can express known problems; independent detection and owner agreement remain unmeasured.
