# Reading packet

Start with [the response form](form.md), which links each commission and story. Read the linked PNG charts with the prose. The intended chart display width is 720px; your actual device, viewport or chart size may differ. Record that size if known. No specific audience is stated in these commissions; say if that limits your response.

You may read fewer stories, skip dimensions, or give only an overall response. Leave skipped items as not rated and state why if possible. The recorded denominator will remain the number of actual responses; missing scores stay null with a reason. Please use your own words. No response is inferred from an empty form.

Use whole numbers from 1–10. 1 and odd numbers are allowed between these guideposts. Overall is an independent judgment, never an average of the three dimensions. These are proposed anchors, not an established owner standard. An 8 does not authorize publication.

| Dimension | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reader value | Dataset tour or familiar facts with little payoff. | A relevant question, but the finding or why it matters remains weak. | A useful finding, but its significance or freshness needs work. | A revealing, consequential comparison or answer worth sharing. | An exceptional, memorable answer that substantially improves the intended reader's understanding. |
| Explanation and argument | The conclusion or relationship between findings is unclear. | An answer is visible, but major reasoning steps or meanings are missing. | Main answer is understandable, but an important explanation, boundary or alternative needs work. | Clear reasoning and explanations; important limits and alternatives sit where they affect interpretation. | Exceptionally clear and convincing reasoning; the relevant mechanisms, alternatives and limits are explained with precision and economy. |
| Presentation | Hard to follow or decode. | Readable in parts, but recurring prose or chart problems obstruct the story. | Understandable, with substantial editing or chart clarification needed. | Fluent prose and charts that make the intended comparison easy to grasp, with only minor edits. | Exceptionally effortless, economical prose and charts that make the story's comparisons immediately clear. |
| Optional independent overall | Confusing or largely a dataset dump. | Understandable, but shallow or weakly explained. | Useful story with substantial editing needed. | Strong, compelling and clear; minor editorial work. | Exceptional reference-quality work. |

Does the proposed 8 / minor-edits anchor fit your own standard? Please respond in the form. This asks about the anchor, not permission to publish. You do not need to recompute data or complete diagnostic or evidence checks.

The reading copies preserve the original article content and charts. Preparation removed only a redundant identical leading title and changed local image destinations to the same existing PNG files; the commissions are byte-identical copies. No charts were regenerated. Neutral A–D names minimize identity cues, but existing public mappings and prior exposure mean this is not proven blind. Record previous familiarity, even if uncertain. A fresh reviewer does not make old stories holdout material.
