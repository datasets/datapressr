# Decisions

- **Monthly key:** Combine source year and month into `date`, represented as `YYYY-MM-01` and used as the primary key. Alternatives were separate year/month fields or a `yearmonth` field. A first-of-month date satisfies the stated date convention and is widely supported; its description explicitly says it denotes a whole month. Profiling found 821 nonblank, unique year/month pairs.
- **Published shape and names:** Publish `data/co2-monthly.csv` as resource `co2-monthly`, titled “Monthly Atmospheric CO2”, with `date`, `decimal_year`, `average_ppm`, `deseasonalized_ppm`, `measurement_days`, `standard_deviation_ppm`, and `uncertainty_ppm`. Alternatives were a mean-only series or retaining redundant year/month columns alongside the date. Keeping every nonredundant source field preserves analytical value and quality information; the chosen names explain the source abbreviations and units. The dataset title is “Mauna Loa Monthly Atmospheric CO2” to identify the place, variable and frequency.
- **Numeric representation:** Validate numbers but write their trimmed source spellings, including trailing zeros. The alternative was converting all values back from JavaScript numbers. Preserving the source spellings retains its visible precision without rounding or reformatting.
- **Missing-value interpretation:** Map `ndays=-1`, `sdev=-9.99`, and `unc=-0.99` to empty cells independently; retain zero and positive values. The alternative was applying one generic negative sentinel list or copying uncertainty's sentinel from the skill examples. Actual snapshot profiling identifies the column-specific markers: 195, 196 and 194 occurrences respectively. Also support the skill's `-99.99` marker for average and deseasonalized CO2, although neither column contains it here. Reject any other negative value instead of silently treating unexpected input as missing.
- **Interpolated and relocated observations:** Retain all source months, including source-interpolated values and the temporary Maunakea observations, and explain both in metadata. Alternatives were dropping these months or adding an inferred interpolation/site flag. The archive does not supply an explicit row-level flag, early Scripps statistics are unavailable for a different reason, and July 2023 spans the return to Mauna Loa. No additional interpolation or classification is performed.
- **Terms attribution:** Use the header's own title, `USE OF NOAA GML DATA`, as the license name, link to the source file, and transcribe its use terms in the license description. Alternatives were assigning CC0, a generic public-domain designation, or another SPDX license. The archive states availability and credit expectations without naming a standard license, so assigning one would assert terms not evidenced by the input. Record both supplied NOAA URLs and the retrieval date; credit the early Scripps contribution in the dataset description.
- **Statistical descriptions:** Describe `sdev` and `unc` conservatively as source standard deviation and uncertainty in ppm, without claiming a confidence level or an uncertainty calculation. The alternative was inferring those details from typical scientific practice; the offline header does not establish them.
- **Build guards and ordering:** Preserve source chronological order and assert the exact header, row width, numeric syntax, valid year/month, nonblank unique key, contiguous months, decimal year within its calendar year, and integer day counts within the calendar month. Also anchor this snapshot to 821 rows and March 1958–July 2026 endpoints. Alternatives were silently sorting/deduplicating, accepting gaps, or avoiding snapshot-specific checks. Failing explicitly exposes changes and truncation; a future snapshot would require deliberately updating the completeness anchors. Resolve input/output paths relative to `build.ts` rather than the caller's working directory so the command behaves consistently.
- **Publication exclusions:** Exclude `archive/`, `scripts/`, `build.ts`, `AGENTS.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, and `.git/` using `.datahubignore`; retain them locally and in version control as applicable. The alternative was publishing build and maintenance files alongside the CSV. Excluding those files keeps the publication focused on its declared resource and metadata while the repository retains reproducibility and decision context.
- **Verification scope:** Use the skill's explicit single-file CSV exemption from the independent-review gate; stripping comment lines and splitting unquoted numeric CSV follows its documented simple-CSV path. Alternatives were invoking the blind-run self-review fallback or treating the standard CSV handling as a layout-dependent custom parser. Neither is necessary here. Beyond the required validator and double build, independently compare every cleaned cell with Python's CSV parser to check column mapping and sentinel handling. Both builds produced SHA-256 `8f7b8ae7a9be302673ecc9a48c19600451e60d5b6525637ce83bcc41336d0e9f`; validation checked 821 rows and 5,747 cells with zero errors and warnings. No additional test framework or review artifact is introduced.
