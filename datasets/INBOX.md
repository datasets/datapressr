- [ ] **Global Social Norms — Everyday Norms** — <https://www.globalsocialnorms.org/everyday-norms> (captured 2026-10-06)
  - **What it is:** a Stockholm-based research initiative. The Everyday Norms study asks how socially appropriate ordinary actions (laughing, eating, singing…) are judged, depending on place and on who is present. 25,422 participants in 90 societies on 6 continents, 2023–2024. Two peer-reviewed papers (2025–2026).
  - **Why it's interesting:** an example of a *website that is a knowledge base where each entry is itself a dataset* — the pattern we want a standard wiki-style way to process. Per catalog-as-repo, this likely becomes its own catalog repo and DataHub publication (possibly under the Commons repo), with each entry a dataset.
  - **Data that might exist:** unknown. The page lists no entry fields, download, API or licence; it points to globalsocialnorms@gmail.com and a "For members" section. Needs a look at licence and access before a `structure` pass.
