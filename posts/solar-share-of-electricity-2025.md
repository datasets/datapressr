# Solar's share of global electricity generation in 2025

**Solar was about 8.7% of global electricity generation in 2025** (up from 6.9% in 2024), per Ember's Global Electricity Review 2026.

Supporting numbers: global solar generation hit a record 2,778 TWh in 2025, up 30% (+636 TWh) from 2024 — the largest single-year increase of any power source ever recorded, and enough to cover about three-quarters of the entire global increase in electricity demand that year. Total renewables (solar + wind + hydro + other) reached 33.8% of global generation. (2,778 TWh ÷ 8.7% ≈ 32,000 TWh total generation, which lines up with independent global generation estimates — sanity-checks fine.)

Worth noting for the idea file: even the most aggressive 1990s/2000s scenarios found earlier topped out around low double digits for solar by 2020–2030 — reality hit 8.7% globally in 2025 and is still accelerating, which reinforces the "underestimated" thesis rather than complicating it.
