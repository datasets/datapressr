# Story skill improvement: current workplan

**The next deliverable is a visibly better complete story and a defensible lesson about how to produce it.** More evaluation records, passing checks or published posts do not by themselves meet that goal. Owner direction on 24 October makes a strong, independently red-teamed skill backlog the main priority; publication of the reviewed temperature story is authorized separately.

This plan is the current map for `datapressr-8no.9`; Beads owns executable tasks and status. Factory's [programme](../../../factory/PLAN.md) links this work through the Story craft and Evaluation and learning initiatives. Reuse the existing harness, tracker, source catalogue and known failures. Do not build another runner or expand a general research survey.

## What “better” means

A reader gains a worthwhile understanding they can explain accurately: a consequential question, a supported finding, a clear line of reasoning and charts that make the comparison easy to see. The owner's reference register is short Guardian/FT/Economist-style data journalism, not a mandatory imitation of any publisher.

Use reader value, explanation/argument and presentation as separate assessments; optional overall remains independent. Keep factual/source, arithmetic, inference, reproduction and delivered-render checks separate from taste. A technically valid but tedious, obvious or unanswered story can fail editorially. Traffic measures attention under a distribution context, not quality or the causal effect of a skill.

## Evidence behind the priority

- [Reference-led results](../../evals/experiments/story-quality/reference-led-v1/results.md): eight outputs passed all seven deterministic checks, none passed every scoped manual gate; four paired primary H1/H2/H3 outcomes tied and runtime preferences split 2–2. Seven writing ratings were 1/2, all interest ratings were 2/2. Those ceilings do not establish compelling writing or improvement.
- [Retention decisions](../../evals/experiments/story-quality/reference-led-v1/decisions.md): unsupported/mismatched explanations and secondary-chart reconstruction remain concrete problems. R1/R2 are provisional; R3 needs a scope-aware candidate. The experiment did not isolate individual rules.
- [Tracker status](../../evals/calibration/story/tracking-v0.1/pilot/STATUS.md): four AI assessments, zero owner ratings and zero new scoped technical checks. This is usable infrastructure, not a calibrated production judge.
- [Owner research](../research/data-story-research.md) and [assessed examples](../research/data-story-exemplar-notes.md): enough material exists to learn specific editorial moves. Source audits are valuable, but we still need to explain what the lede, sequence, chart, counterpoint and ending do for the reader and then apply that learning.
- The [temperature story](https://datahub.io/blog/2026-temperature-record-hurdle) is exposed production material, not a fresh test case. It offers a useful development question: can orientation and significance arrive sooner while retaining correct conditional arithmetic? Publication approval is not an owner quality score. Work on a separate copy; never replace the live story silently.

## Three lanes, with craft first

### A. Bounded editorial workshop — main priority

Epic **`datapressr-8no.9.13`**, primary Factory initiative Story craft. Review boundary **7 November**: complete the bounded outcome or explicitly replan; do not automatically extend a sprint until it produces a win.

| Order | Task | Concrete output | Readiness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `.9.13.1` Dissect craft moves and choose a stronger commission | Two short reference dissections; three evidence-feasible angles for the exposed temperature story; one selected brief with artifact pins and predicted reader improvement | P0, runnable now; one 90-minute author session and one brief review |
| 2 | `.9.13.2` Complete editorial workshop | Complete original/revised story, independently read and fact/render checked; useful-improvement, mixed or no-improvement decision | P0, after `.13.1`; at most two 90-minute author sessions and two 45-minute reader/reviewer sessions, maximum two revision rounds |
| 3 | `.9.13.3` Lesson to candidate/transfer proposal | At most two reusable lessons, negative examples, a minimal candidate diff and bounded next-test proposal; or explicit no-change decision | P1, after `.13.2`; one 90-minute synthesis session and one review |

The first artifact is a whole reading experience, not only a nicer heading or a new scoring rubric. Deliver original/revised rendered reader packets: reuse byte-identical charts when unchanged, render any changed charts at the actual reading sizes, and retain those exact delivered assets. A chart plan alone cannot establish comprehension or reduced reading burden. Question choice/reframing is part of the workshop: compare three angles for reader payoff, available evidence, unanswered claims and effort. The commissioned question may narrow honestly; do not substitute a more answerable question without recording it. Changing the question limits attribution to any single writing rule.

Study two already assessed accessible examples, not another catalogue. Explain precisely how paragraph/chart order, stakes, comparator, explanatory bridge, counterevidence or ending works; apply selected moves. No lengthy quotations or private-book dependency. If an example cannot be accessed, use another existing assessed accessible example and record the limitation.

The independent reader sees randomized artifact labels without author rationale or prior ratings, records prior exposure, gives a spontaneous main takeaway, explains what changed their understanding, identifies the weakest passage and prefers A/B/neither with reasons. The reviewer then checks central evidence and actual delivered-size charts. If prior exposure prevents meaningful masking, label the reading unmasked exploratory and do not manufacture a blind comparison. Record the exact versions and scope once using existing tracker/schema where applicable; workshop development records may remain a small pinned directory, not a new platform.

**Local improvement decision:** the revised main takeaway is accurate, no material source/inference/render regression is found, and the independent reader gives an evidence-backed editorial preference. Mixed judgments or caveat-only changes are not an unqualified win. AI preference is not owner taste, calibration, statistical significance or general skill efficacy. Offer one small optional owner A/B reaction; missing feedback does not halt reversible development or become agreement.

A second contrasting exposed France/oil case is **conditional**, proposed only if the first yields a plausible lesson. Keep it to one pass with a stated question and cap; no automatic second draft or inference batch. If the first produces no useful lesson within its cap, retain the evidence, close the attempt and choose a different hypothesis explicitly.

Rules to challenge only where evidence warrants: every number on a chart, obligatory chart-first opening, fixed length, rigidity after outline approval and the current claim that approximately 6px mobile labels are fine. These are possible constraints, not established causes. Development copies may vary a rule with a declared rationale and affected-scope review; the installed skill is not silently relaxed. If the problem is simply a clipping bug, fix/check that defect rather than invent a new editorial theory. Keep facts, counterevidence and traceability even when simplifying presentation.

Candidate authoring no longer waits for completion of a case the author must not see. Only opaque identifiers/readiness attestations cross that boundary until the author is frozen. The final comparison still needs all three prerequisites. Historical artifacts stay immutable; any exposure loss becomes explicit exploratory status. No candidate may be revised using fresh-case findings and still called a fresh test.

R3 must improve explanation, not just add “the data cannot say” everywhere. Preserve qualified external mechanisms; an unsupported causal story fails even if readers find it compelling. Its controlled test measures drafting given a fixed question/source packet; it does **not** measure commissioning, source discovery or reframing. The workshop covers those development questions separately. Do not mix workshop changes into the R3 candidate.

Before execution, the proposal must pin writer/baseline/candidate identities, requested/served model evidence, cases, pair/order design, main comprehension outcome, source/inference gates, retries, missing-data treatment and aggregate call/token/time/cost ceilings with an enforcement/stop procedure. No automatic extra replicate or rescue rewrite after seeing outcomes. No paid batch is authorized by this plan. Prefer OpenAI agents; a different vendor is not itself proof of independence. A no-effect or inconclusive result is a valid stop.

## Backlog ordering and stop rules

**First assign `.9.13.1`.** It yields the brief for actual craft work. In parallel, one isolated agent can take `.9.9` or `.9.14`; avoid opening every ready task. Keep only one active development-story revision and one active controlled candidate. Case preparation is lower priority until the candidate path is credible. `hcn.15` regression automation remains P3 after its existing calibration gates; its old paid-run estimate is not fresh execution authorization. Structure/enrich evaluations, private-book access, a broad new source survey, more genres and new score dimensions remain outside this cycle.

Timeboxes are maximum work allocations, not measured performance estimates. Record actual elapsed time/usage where tools supply it; unknown costs remain unknown. Reaching a cap produces a retained artifact/decision or an explicit replan, never automatic continuation. No scheduled background work is created.

Each completed task records: concrete artifact and hash/commit; exact materials/exposure; checks and reader evidence; resource coverage; retain/revise/reject/no-change decision; next action and owner. Independent review uses a fresh context and does not share authorship. Recheck affected science/rendering after material changes, without repeating every historical audit for a wording correction.

A lesson becomes a proposed skill edit only after a concrete before/after and negative example justify it. A candidate becomes installed guidance only through a separately reviewed adoption task with declared evidence limits. One edited story can justify a useful local practice, not a universal quality claim. Keep provisionally sensible advice distinct from experimentally supported effects.
