# DataPressr brand and positioning

Working brand reference, grounded in the owner’s clarification on 2026-10-01. DataPressr is skills and an app for wrangling data and making data-driven stories in the AI age. This document records positioning; Beads remains the actionable task queue.

## Core description

**DataPressr is a suite of skills and tools for wrangling data and creating data-driven stories with AI.**

The suite includes reusable skills for agents and an app that brings the conversation and the outputs together. The broader ambition is to improve how people work with data in the AI age: investigating sources, preparing datasets, finding insight and communicating it.

Turning raw data into insight is the audience’s underlying activity. An opening that only describes that activity is too generic to explain DataPressr. Introduce the audience briefly, say what we are building, and show the specific part of the suite being demonstrated.

## The suite and the app

| Level | How to describe it |
|---|---|
| DataPressr | Skills and tools for data wrangling and data-driven storytelling with AI. |
| Skills | Reusable instructions and workflows that guide an agent through the work. |
| App | An integrated surface for working with an AI while viewing the data, documentation, charts and stories. |
| Current app prototype | An early implementation inside BB. This demo shows the app part of DataPressr. |

BB is the current prototype host. Mention it when explaining where this implementation runs; the product story should center on the integrated working experience. Avoid making DataPressr sound synonymous with BB or with one preview plugin.

The current viewer is read-only. The agent changes project files, and the preview updates. Broader ambitions should be described as direction; show specific current capabilities as evidence.

## A useful analogy

The owner’s shorthand is **“like Claude Design, but for data-driven insight.”** Use this as a conversational analogy for working with AI alongside a visible result. It is an analogy, not an assertion of feature parity or affiliation. Follow it with what the app actually does: conversation, data and stories in one working surface.

## App demo opening

Suggested copy for the next script revision:

> If you wrangle data or tell stories with it, we’re building DataPressr for you: skills and tools for working with AI.
>
> Today we’re showing the app. Your conversation with an agent, beside the data, documents and charts you’re working on.
>
> We’ve built this prototype inside BB. Let’s open a dataset.

Then move immediately to the CSV and the useful edit/result moment. Keep general context short. The demo should let someone understand this specific integrated surface before covering the rest of the suite.

This replaces the positioning direction of the v2 opening, which framed a generic data-to-story ambition as the DataPressr vision. The preserved v2 export and its source remain historical artifacts; this proposed opening has not been recorded or rendered.

## Voice and tone

Friendly, direct, specific and lightly funny. Explain the product as a colleague showing something useful. Use ordinary language, concrete work and visible results. A small dry aside is enough; jokes should not require additional scenes or explanations. PostHog’s conversational tone is a reference, not a requirement to mimic its vocabulary or persona.

For narration, leave time to inspect the screen. Put pauses around the transition from context to demonstration and before the changed result. A faster read is not automatically a more engaging demo. Select a voice and performance with warmth, varied emphasis and natural pauses; avoid an announcement-style delivery.
