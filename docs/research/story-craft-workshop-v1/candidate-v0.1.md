## Original synthetic example and boundaries

**Threshold before purpose:** “The final quarter needs an average of 160 orders a month. The calculation gives each month equal weight.”

**Question and relationship first:** “Matching last year's strongest quarter would still leave the shop short of its annual target. Its first nine months averaged 80 orders a month; the last three would need 160 to reach the full-year target of 100.” Follow with the chart showing that gap and the historical comparator. These invented values illustrate the move: `(9 × 80 + 3 × 160) / 12 = 100`; suppose last year's strongest quarter averaged 140. This is arithmetic about a target, not a sales forecast.

**Do not write:** “Even a record quarter cannot save the year.” A quarter averaging more than 160 could exceed the target; “record” does not mean “matching the previous record”. Nor should a chart that already shows the annual shortfall acquire a redundant paragraph listing every measurement definition. The aim is a clear relationship, not more words before every figure.

The temperature revision's exact move was “Only three months remain to offset a deficit built up over nine,” before “What would it take to close the gap?” Its opening definition paragraph remained a reader-reported weakness, and the original had the stronger immediate desktop visual. Preserve those counterexamples when judging this candidate. The bounded exposed-oil proposal and stopping conditions are in the decision; no transfer result or adoption is claimed here.
